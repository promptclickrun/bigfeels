# bigfeels brand

The mark links two rounded forms into a lowercase b, with a separate dot for a piece of evidence. The website pairs the mark with an outlined Manrope wordmark.

## Files

- bigfeels-logo.svg: primary plum wordmark and icon, with text converted to paths
- bigfeels-logo-light.svg: light wordmark for dark backgrounds
- bigfeels-icon.svg: standalone scalable mark
- bigfeels-logo.png and bigfeels-icon.png: transparent raster versions
- icon-512.png: lavender-backed square icon
- apple-touch-icon.png: touch icon
- favicon.ico: 16, 32, and 48 pixel variants

## Palette

Plum #241633
Lavender #b5a0ee
Paper #f7f5fa
Citron #e7ff87

Use the plum mark on light backgrounds and the light wordmark on plum. Keep clear space equal to at least the dot diameter. Preserve the original proportions and avoid decorative effects. The site uses the mark at 37 pixels and above; the favicon includes dedicated small raster exports.
